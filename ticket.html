Ticketing Systems - What are they?

Details

Tickets are what engineers use to identify an issue and track it. It's a way to compartmentalize each inidividual issue in a way that's easy to track and refer to. However it can get pretty overwhelming when dozens of people submit tickets, each of which could do with unrelated issues, applications, services and vendors. Ticketing Systems aim to eliminate that headache; they organize tickets in a view that's easy to understand, and add identifying traits to make them easier to update, track, and complete. Some ticketing systems also integrate ways to make relating articles accessible to both the engineer and the person submitting the ticket, that way they can resolve the issue faster, sometimes without the engineer ever having to look at it!

Example

The ticketing system that I've used for 4 years now at 2 different companies is Jira by Atlassan. Jira does ticket sorting and organizing very well. They display information in clear seperated blocks in ways that delivers information organized and efficiently. Jira automatically numbers incoming tickets, supports braching & linking tickets for related issues, allows for easy duplications for recurring issues, and even supports scripting with MSFT Office to automatically assign a ticket to an engineer as they come in. Jira is managed by Atlassan, who also has a support documentation branch named Confluance. These apps flow seamlessly with eachother, making support and documentation as easy as possible, as long as the work is done to set both sites up.
